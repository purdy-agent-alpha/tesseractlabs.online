Tesseract Labs Connector Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026. Updated 21 October 2026.

Tesseract Labs Connector ("the service") is a personal assistant operated by an individual ("we", "the operator"). Today it connects only the operator's own accounts, and it is not open for sign-up. Before anyone else's account is connected, this policy will be updated and they will be asked to agree to it. This policy explains what data the service accesses when an account is connected, how it is used, where it goes, and how it is removed.

1. Data we access

The service accesses only the accounts you connect, and only through the permissions you grant on Google's consent screen.

  • Email (Gmail). With "View your email messages and settings" the service can read message headers (sender, recipients, subject, date), plain-text message bodies, attachment names and labels. With "Send email on your behalf", which is requested only if you want the assistant to send for you, it can send messages that you have approved.
  • Future integrations (calendar, contacts, files). These will be requested separately, read-only, and only if you choose to connect them. This policy will be updated before any of them is requested.
  • Account identifier. Your email address, to tell your connected accounts apart.

We do not access attachment contents, passwords, or payment information. Messages that contain sign-in codes, password-reset or verification links, or credentials are automatically withheld. The assistant sees only that such a message arrived, from whom, when, and what kind of message it is.

2. How we use it

Only to provide the features you use:

  • answering your questions about your own messages (and later your calendar, contacts and files);
  • preparing summaries and briefings for you;
  • keeping a private index of the people you correspond with, so you can find and keep in touch with them;
  • composing messages you ask for, and sending each one only after you have approved its full text.

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it, or build profiles for anyone else. Who else handles it is set out in section 4, and when people read it in section 5.

3. Storage and security

  • Data from your accounts, and the summaries and index made from it, are stored on a computer the operator controls. Some records made from them, such as the list of people you correspond with, are also kept in a private GitHub repository (section 4).
  • Your Google sign-in tokens are held by a separate component on that computer. The assistant cannot read them.
  • A backup of that computer is kept on storage in the operator's home.
  • Access to the computer is restricted to the operator. The assistant's actions are logged and supervised.

4. Who else handles your data

  • AI model providers. To answer you, the assistant sends them the messages, or the parts of them, that it needs. Today these are Anthropic, which keeps them for up to 30 days (longer where its safety systems flag them, to resolve disputes, or where the law requires) and, under the account's settings, does not train its models on them; and models reached through OpenRouter, used only on routes that keep no data and do not collect it.
  • GitHub, which hosts the private repository where the assistant keeps some records it makes from your data.
  • Telegram. The assistant's answers, briefings and the messages it asks you to approve reach you in Telegram chats, which Telegram stores on its servers.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

No other third parties receive your data.

5. Google API Services: Limited Use

Tesseract Labs Connector's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features of the service. It is not transferred except as described above, used for advertising, or sold. It is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. People read it only with your consent, for security, to comply with law, or when the data has been aggregated and anonymized.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Data stored by the service is kept until you ask for it to be deleted.
  • You can revoke the service's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The service then stops accessing your account, but what it has already stored stays until you ask for deletion.
  • To have your stored data and index deleted, write to purdy1agent1alpha@gmail.com. We delete it from the service within 30 days. Older backups may still hold a copy; they stay on the operator's own storage and the service does not use them.

7. Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Before using data in a new way or requesting a new permission, we will tell connected users and ask for consent again.
