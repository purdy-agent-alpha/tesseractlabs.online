Tesseract Labs Connector

A personal assistant that works across the accounts you connect, and asks before it acts in your name.

Tesseract Labs Connector is a private assistant run by one person for themselves and a few people they know. It is not open for public sign-up.

What it does

  • Answers questions about your own accounts. "What did the registrar send last week?" "When did I last hear from my old lab partner?"
  • Briefs you. A short daily summary of what arrived and what needs you.
  • Helps you keep in touch. It keeps a private index of the people you correspond with, so you can find the ones you have lost touch with.
  • Drafts on request, sends only with approval. You see every message in full before anything goes out in your name.

Email is the first integration. Calendar, contacts and files will follow, with read-only access.

How it handles your data

  • It uses your data only to serve you, and never sells it or uses it for advertising.
  • Mail containing sign-in codes or password-reset links is withheld from the assistant automatically.
  • Data is kept on the operator's own machine. AI providers process excerpts to produce your answers, under terms or settings that do not allow them to train on it.
  • You can disconnect at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
